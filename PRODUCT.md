# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js + Tailwind CSS (user-specified; matches the recommended stack for this multi-page app).

## Users

Primary audiences, both confirmed:
- **Medical travelers**: people researching and arranging treatment abroad — surgery, specialty care, recovery, and travel/aftercare — who want vetted coordination for a high-stakes decision.
- **Healthcare providers/institutions**: hospitals and clinics marketing to international patients and seeking to be listed, packaged, and booked through the platform.

## Product Purpose

A medical tourism and healthcare concierge web application that connects international patients with vetted providers and packages, and walks each patient through a coordinated journey — inquiry, telemedicine consultation, travel, treatment, and aftercare. Success means a patient can discover a specialty and package, submit an inquiry with supporting documents, and book a timezone-aware telemedicine consult, guided by a clear 3-phase timeline.

## Positioning

A single coordinated concierge path from discovery to aftercare — one platform that pairs patient-side orchestration (inquiry + telemedicine booking + journey timeline) with a provider catalog of specialties and packages — rather than a bare directory or a single clinic's site.

## Operating Context

Web app used across the patient journey and the provider/program side:
- Multi-step inquiry modal with file upload of medical documents.
- Timezone-aware telemedicine booking workflow.
- A 3-phase patient journey timeline (discovery/planning → coordination/travel → treatment/aftercare; exact phase names to be confirmed).
- Multi-page navigation across home, specialties, packages, blog, and per-item detail pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed scope:
- Dynamic routes: `/specialties/[slug]`, `/packages/[slug]`, `/blog/[slug]`.
- Multi-step inquiry modal with file upload.
- Timezone-aware telemedicine booking.
- Clear 3-phase patient journey timeline.
- Clean, accessible interface.

Explicitly undecided/open (do not fabricate): exact specialty/package catalog contents, provider listings, pricing, accreditations, and organizational identity.

## Brand Commitments

None confirmed. This is a concept/placeholder — invent placeholder branding (name, voice, palette) and label non-verified claims as placeholders rather than real proof.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. All medical claims, credentials, accreditation, testimonials, provider listings, and pricing are placeholder/inferred and must be clearly labeled as such, never presented as verified fact.

## Product Principles

- **Trust under medical stakes**: convey credibility and reassurance without ever inventing real credentials or fabricated patient proof.
- **Coordinate, don't just list**: the platform's value is the guided journey and concierge workflow, not a single directory page.
- **Reduce decision friction**: clear specialties/packages, an easy multi-step inquiry, and a timezone-aware booking flow.
- **Accessible and clean**: a legible, inclusive interface suited to a stressful, high-stakes decision.
- **Concept-first**: placeholder branding and content keep the door open for a real client or real organization later.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessible by design (semantic markup, keyboard-usable multi-step forms, clear labels/errors). No stricter standard was confirmed.
